# Defending Intellectual Property

## Statement

Dear readers,

Since the creation of the **Advanced‑Java** project, we have received much feedback and worked hard to improve its contents.  Unfortunately, plagiarism and copyright infringement are common on the internet.  We cannot allow such behaviour to go unchecked.

Therefore we make the following declaration: apart from the official sources—[doocs/advanced‑java](https://github.com/doocs/advanced-java) and the author’s blog “**Shishan’s Architecture Notes**”—if you find articles on other platforms that are identical or very similar to our content but **do not provide proper attribution or even claim to be original**, please report them.  Feel free to share the links to infringing articles here so they can be exposed.  Thank you for your support.

We hope everyone protects intellectual property and respects others’ work so that we can build a healthy knowledge‑sharing ecosystem together.
