# Microservice Governance

Governance ensures that a microservice ecosystem remains maintainable and secure. It includes:

- **API gateway** – a single entry point for authentication, rate limiting and routing.
- **Configuration management** – centralise configuration for dynamic updates.
- **Observability** – logging, metrics and distributed tracing to diagnose issues.
- **Security** – enforce authentication, authorisation and encryption across services.

Strong governance prevents chaos as the number of services grows.
