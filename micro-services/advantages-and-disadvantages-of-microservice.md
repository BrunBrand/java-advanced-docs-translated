# Advantages and Disadvantages of Microservices

**Advantages**:

- **Independent deployment** – services can be developed, deployed and scaled independently.
- **Technology diversity** – each service can use the best language or framework for its domain.
- **Fault isolation** – failures in one service do not necessarily bring down the entire system.
- **Scalability** – services can scale horizontally based on demand.

**Disadvantages**:

- **Operational complexity** – many services require automation, monitoring and deployment tooling.
- **Distributed transactions** – maintaining consistency across services is hard.
- **Network latency and failure** – calls between services add latency and can fail.
- **Team coordination** – defining service boundaries and responsibilities requires organisational discipline.
