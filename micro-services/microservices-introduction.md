# Introduction to Microservices

Microservices are a response to the limitations of monolithic architectures. Instead of building a single large application, microservices break functionality into **small, autonomous services** that communicate via well‑defined APIs. Characteristics include:

- **Independent deployment** – each service can be updated without redeploying the entire system.
- **Business‑oriented services** – align service boundaries with business capabilities.
- **Decentralised governance** – teams choose appropriate technologies.
