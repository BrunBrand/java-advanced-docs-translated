# Choosing a Microservice Deployment Strategy

Deployment options for microservices include:

- **Single process per service** – deploy each service in its own process or container (e.g., Docker). Simplifies dependency management and scaling.
- **Service mesh** – use infrastructure like Istio to handle routing, observability and resilience outside the application code.
- **Serverless** – deploy functions for lightweight services; ideal for event‑driven workloads.

Consider factors like team skill, infrastructure cost and operational overhead when selecting a strategy.
