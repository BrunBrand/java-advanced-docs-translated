# Doocs Official Account

The Doocs open‑source community runs an official public account called **“Doocs”** on WeChat.  It focuses on exploring IT knowledge and helping developers grow.  This translation summarises the key points of the original Chinese page.

## Why follow the official account?

WeChat’s public‑account platform has strong **original‑content protection** and makes it convenient to read articles.  Going forward, all original articles by the Doocs team will be published on the “Doocs” account.  If other account owners wish to reprint articles, please send a message via the public account or contact the author directly on WeChat (ID: YLB0109).

## What content does the account provide?

The account aims to:

- **High‑quality content** – articles will not simply copy and paste random posts from the internet.  Even reprints of technical articles will be selected carefully.
- **Regular updates** – the goal is to publish one or two articles per week to maintain a consistent schedule.

The original page contains a QR code for subscribing and a sample image of the article layout.

## Is there a chat group?

Yes.  The WeChat group “Doocs Tech Friends” has nearly 200 members.  If you’d like to join, contact the author on WeChat.  The group focuses on sharing development knowledge.  Advertisements, promotional links and spam are strictly prohibited.
