# Let Us Progress Together

> **Let those who hurt heal, let those who struggle find hope.**

This piece combines a short message of encouragement with a longer poem.  The embedded music player in the original page has been omitted.

## Song Translation

```
The moment of farewell has arrived;
pack some happiness in your luggage.

Ahead is covered by dark clouds;
the struggle in my heart, like a trapped beast growling low.

A vague silence
reveals a bit of bitterness.
May you no longer be hurt;
do not fall asleep with tears.

In this twisted world
answers are not necessarily absolute;
wounds become heroic because of love.
Let us advance together.

The power of rebirth comes from the true self;
defeat respectable opponents, yeah~

Holding onto faith is all I have;
reach the vastness in my heart, yeah~

What has been lost needs to progress,
what is regretted needs to progress,
what you can’t let go of needs to progress, yeah~

The light of the sanctuary
guides the direction, oh~

After crossing the catastrophe,
dawn gradually lights up, oh~

Hope is shattered by struggle;
then there is nothing more to ask for—thus free.

A lonely hero facing the wind
also longs to dock, but cannot say it.

… (chorus repeats)

The song repeats its themes of perseverance, healing and self‑improvement, urging listeners to overcome challenges and move forward together.
```

Although poetic, the song emphasizes resilience: even when life seems dark and full of obstacles, hold on to your convictions, heal your wounds and continue to “advance” so that you can emerge stronger.
