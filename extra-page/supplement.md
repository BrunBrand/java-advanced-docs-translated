# Project Supplements

This folder provides supplemental materials for the **Advanced‑Java** project, including motivational songs, statements on intellectual property, and information about our official public account.  The original Chinese pages have been translated here to help English readers understand the context.

## Offer and Progress

- **Where is my offer?**  This playful song expresses the anxieties of job hunting.  It encourages candidates to work hard, stay disciplined, and avoid distractions like games and TV shows while preparing for interviews.
- **Let us progress together!**  A poetic piece that comforts the listener.  It urges people who are hurt to heal and those who struggle to find hope, then describes overcoming difficulties and striving for self‑improvement.

We value knowledge and respect the labour of content creators. ## Official Account

**Doocs** is the only official public account of the Doocs community.  It focuses on sharing high‑quality technical articles and keeping readers informed about industry news.  You can subscribe to the account or add the author on WeChat (mention “GitHub”) to join our technical discussion group.

Scan the QR codes on the original page to follow the public account or connect with the author.  By replying “PDF” in the Doocs account, you will receive an offline PDF version of the project (283 pages) for easier reading.
