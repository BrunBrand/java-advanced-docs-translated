# Where Is My Offer?

This playful song mirrors the anxieties of a job seeker preparing for technical interviews at major internet companies.  In the original page it was accompanied by images and an embedded music player, which have been removed here.

## Song Translation

```
Every day the first sentence after waking up
is to cheer myself up.
Every time I refresh to check my test scores,
I say “bless me”.
Mirror, mirror on the wall, where is my offer?
I must work hard, I want to become a harvester.

offer, offer—
I want to become a harvester;
offer, offer…

To land a job at BAT* I hold my breath every day;
I send my résumé to big and small companies,
practise interview questions and coding tests.
Though talented, every interview still ends in failure.
I must work hard, I want to become a harvester—wow.

Where is it? Where is it? Where?
Where is it? Where is it? Where?
Where is my offer?
Burn my calories.

Good‑bye to anime, variety shows and movies;
take them away, don’t be polite.

Good‑bye to games like Honor of Kings and PUBG;
stop gaming all night and harming my health.

Come on, calm down,
pick up your textbooks again,
do real interview questions on Niuke.com,
soak goji berries in a thermos.

Come on, take a deep breath,
practise in front of the mirror;
simulate one‑on‑one and panel interviews,
don’t stop until you get an offer.

…

*BAT refers to Baidu, Alibaba and Tencent—China’s leading tech companies.
```

This humorous song motivates job seekers to stay disciplined and keep practising.  It playfully describes quitting various distractions (cartoons, entertainment shows, gaming) to focus on studying, doing mock interviews and maintaining healthy habits, all for the goal of getting an offer from a big tech firm.
